## Other findings

### F1 The 10,000-tick soak ends with one building and proves less than its line suggests

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Evidence:** `scripts/soak.ts:153` "if (script.length - refused < 20) fail(`only ${script.length - refused} commands were carried out`);"
- **Case:** Pass 1's bot clears lots as often as it builds. It ends with `buildings=1`, and 355 of its 402 commands were refused. Its only coverage gates are 20 commands carried out and 3 kinds built. Nothing requires a pin, a cancel, a method bought or switched, an open-slot change, or a leaving or arrival. For most of its 10,000 ticks the invariants run on a near-empty town. Its `check()` also skips 4 invariants: queue targets alive, ids unique, `carry` under 10,000 and `debt` under 100. A queue job pointing at a removed building would crash `construct` (`packages/sim/src/build.ts:240`) before the soak named it.
- **Fix:** Count the carried-out commands per type, and fail when `pin`, `cancelJob`, `setMethod` or `setOpen` has none. Require a floor for peak buildings and peak residents. Make `clear` rarer. Add the 4 invariants to `check()`.

### F2 The purity check can pass without scanning anything

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Evidence:** `scripts/check-sim-purity.mjs:90` "if (import.meta.url === `file://${process.argv[1]}`) {"
- **Case:** The scan runs only when the script's URL equals `file://` plus `argv[1]` character for character. A checkout path with a space, or another character that URLs encode, or a symlinked path, skips the whole scan and exits 0. `npm run check` then passes with the D-002 guard off. Today it prints "purity: 16 files clean". Its banned list also lacks `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `localStorage`, `navigator`, `eval` and `Math.hypot`, `Math.atan2` and `Math.cbrt`. `Math['random']` and `const { random } = Math` get past it. That leaves D-003 (no runtime network) unguarded in the engine.
- **Fix:** Compare against `pathToFileURL(process.argv[1]).href`, and fail when 0 files were scanned. Add the missing names to the list.

### F3 Sheets don't keep or return keyboard focus, have no name, and are tested by mouse only

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Evidence:** `apps/web/src/ui/Sheet.tsx:27` "aria-modal="
- **Case:** Measured at 412 px:
  - With the Forager's hut sheet open, 3 presses of Tab move focus out of the sheet to the Town and People tabs and the lots behind it.
  - Escape closes the sheet, but focus lands on the page body, not on the lot that opened it.
  - The dialog has no `aria-labelledby`, so a screen reader announces an unnamed dialog.
  - The tab bar marks the current tab with `aria-current="page"` (`apps/web/src/App.tsx:420`), which is meant for links.
  - No browser test presses Escape or walks a sheet by keyboard. Only `tests/browser/smoke.spec.ts:162` presses Enter.

  This matters little on a touch phone, but every later screen reuses `Sheet`.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep focus inside the sheet while it is open.
  - Return focus to the opener on close.
  - Label the dialog by its title.
  - Mark the tabs with `aria-pressed`.
  - Add one browser test: open a sheet, Tab through it, press Escape, and check where focus lands.
