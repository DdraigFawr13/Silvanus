## Lots and growth

- **P1** Every lot on the map is a button that opens its sheet. Tapping the map is the main way to play. · D-041 · chat after the mock-up, 2026-11-14 · "also i need to be able to click lots on the map. I want that to be the main way to like be able to interact with things"
- **P2** Empty lots carry a soft pale glow. · D-041 · canvas rounds 2 and 4 2026-11-14 · "the lots need to have a better glow" · "I want the empty lots to have a pale glow"
- **P3** Built lots show no glow until tapped, then glow gold. · D-041 · canvas round 4 2026-11-14 · "I don't want the lots to constantly glow like that"
- **P4** An empty plot carries a Build marker that opens the build sheet. (Claude's words) · D-041 decision text
- **P5** Built lots have dressed yards: doorsteps, worn paths and props that belong to that building. · D-041 · canvas round 4 2026-11-14 · "add more richness around the buildings that are built"
- **P6** Staked future lots show where the Village will grow: roped plots of fallow ground with a sign. · D-041 · canvas round 7 2026-11-14 · "Upgrade the looks of future lot they don't read right"
- **P7** Day one is open: buildings on grass. Each building gains its garden, fence, washing line, woodpile and flower boxes as it is built and upgraded, until the grown town is dense. · D-047 · layout1 form 2026-11-14 · "Start open (B) and fill in towards dense (A) as you build and upgrade"
- **P8** Each upgrade visibly improves the building. Tier 2 adds a stone footing, window boxes, a fenced yard and a lean-to; tier 3 builds bigger with a garden, a hedge and lanterns. · D-041 · canvas rounds 3 and 5 2026-11-14 · "the buildings did not seem to change enough" · "I want upgrades to make things look nicer"
- **P9** Lots are easy to tell apart. · art2 form 2026-11-14
- **P10** The land is one painted picture, and buildings are separate pieces the game adds as they are built. · Workshop log 2026-11-16 · "I just want a canvas style map I can scroll left and right on on my phone with assets and blending you do in pixel lab ... you create building blocks and then blend things together"
- **P11** The town moves: residents walk between home, work and the Hall, working buildings smoke, builds show scaffolding, settlers come and go on the road. · D-037 · M2 re-plan 2026-11-14 · "I just like seeing some animation showing things happening"
