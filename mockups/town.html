Choices drawn here that the spec left open

  • The stock bar is 4 cells across, as the style doc says, so the fifth resource (Tools) sits alone on a second row. Five across would fit one row but makes each cell about 70 px wide at 412 px.
  • No tab bar yet. The style doc shows only tabs that work, and the People tab arrives in S4.
  • The Hall row shows "Residents 8 / 25" so the town's size is on screen before the People tab exists.
  • A job that has not started reads "Waiting, takes 1 game day"; its lot reads "Waiting in the work queue".
  • The Tavern can be built from S2 but does nothing until S5, when morale arrives. Its line says only "Work for 1".
