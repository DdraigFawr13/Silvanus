Town tab with the M2b art

Mock-up for M2c (the town view) and the art picked in the M2b workshop: Kenney Tiny Town and Tiny Dungeon tiles (CC0) repainted in the Ashwick palette (70% Moss, 30% Faded ink), a wide town that scrolls left and right with the Hall in the centre, the "now" bar pinned under the stock, icons in the stock bar, and picture headers on the board cards. Shown at 412 px and 360 px. The town is a still picture here; residents walk and smoke rises in M2c.
